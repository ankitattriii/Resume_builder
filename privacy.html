Privacy

This resume builder runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, and we do not run a server that receives your resume.

What is stored, and where

Your resumes, including any photo you add, are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data, or pressing "Delete my data" in the tool, removes them. Use "Backup" to save a copy to a file you control.

What we do not do

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts and no third-party fonts or services are loaded by this page.

Hosting logs

The website host may keep ordinary server logs, such as your IP address and the time of your visit, as most hosts do. These logs do not include anything you type into the tool.

Downloads

PDF, Word and text files are created on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Shared computers

If you use a shared or public computer, press "Delete my data" before you leave.
